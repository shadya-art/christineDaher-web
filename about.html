A Little About My Journey as a Resilience Practitioner

I am Christine Daher, an ICF-certified Transformative Coach, Health Coach, and NLP Practitioner, with a special focus on supporting healthcare professionals.

I spent more than ten years working in healthcare as an intensive care nurse. This experience gave me a deep understanding of the pressures and challenges healthcare professionals face every day.

Today, I combine my healthcare experience with evidence-informed coaching, mindset strategies, and behavioral tools to help my clients create meaningful and sustainable change.
